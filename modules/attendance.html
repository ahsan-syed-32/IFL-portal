Source: your monthly attendance Excel files, stored in this browser. “A” days are split into Leave (from the sheet’s Leave column) and Absent. Incomplete punches count as present but get no hours or overtime. Overtime is taken from the Overtime column of your monthly sheet; if it is empty, it is calculated from punch times (time after off time + grace).
